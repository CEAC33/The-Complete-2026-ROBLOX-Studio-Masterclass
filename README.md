### Tool Settings and Collisions

- Snap to grid - allow you to control how much parts are moved by the three tools
- Stud - small square, unit of measurement in Roblox
- Degrees - 360 degrees of a circle
- You can turn on/off or adjust the steps in movement
- Collisions - stops you from moving inside another part, good practice to have it on
- Degree steps - keep it to a factor of 360
